  - heading: Who is responsible
    body: |
      This website is operated by **Florencia Sol Risé López** (Sol Risé Soprano), an independent artist.

      For questions about this policy or your personal data, contact us at [solrisesoprano@gmail.com](mailto:solrisesoprano@gmail.com).
  - heading: What data we collect
    body: |
      We collect only what is needed to run the site and respond to enquiries:

      * **Contact form** — name, email address, subject and message, when you choose to send them.
      * **Language preference** — stored in your browser (`localStorage`) if you switch language or dismiss the language suggestion.
      * **Server logs** — our hosting provider may record technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and access time (standard web server logs).
  - heading: How we use your data
    body: |
      * **Contact messages** — to read and reply to your enquiry.
      * **Language preference** — to remember your chosen language on future visits.
      * **Server logs** — to keep the site secure, diagnose errors and maintain performance.

      We do **not** use your data for advertising profiles, sell your data, or send marketing emails.
  - heading: Legal basis (EEA visitors)
    body: |
      If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, we rely on:

      * **Consent** — when you submit the contact form.
      * **Legitimate interest** — to operate and protect the website, and to remember functional preferences (language).
      * **Contract / pre-contractual steps** — when you contact us about artistic engagements or collaborations.
  - heading: How long we keep data
    body: |
      * **Contact form submissions** — kept as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any follow-up, then deleted or archived unless we have a legal reason to keep them longer.
      * **Language preference** — until you clear your browser storage or we change how the site works.
      * **Server logs** — according to our host’s retention settings (typically a limited period).
  - heading: Third-party services
    body: |
      * **Netlify** — hosts the website and processes contact form submissions on our behalf ([Netlify Privacy Policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/)).
      * **YouTube** — when you **play a video** embedded on this site (for example in the gallery or schedule), Google/YouTube may set cookies or collect usage data under their own terms ([Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)). Videos are not loaded until you open them.
      * **External links** — social profiles (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Muvac) open on third-party sites with their own policies.
  - heading: Your rights
    body: |
      Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal data, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

      To exercise these rights, email [solrisesoprano@gmail.com](mailto:solrisesoprano@gmail.com). We will respond within a reasonable time.
  - heading: Cookies and local storage
    body: |
      This site does **not** set analytics or advertising cookies.

      We use **local storage** only for:

      * your language preference (`solrise-locale`);
      * whether you dismissed the language suggestion banner (`solrise-locale-suggest-dismissed`).

      These are functional settings, not used to track you across other websites. No cookie consent banner is required for this use today. If we add analytics or non-essential cookies in the future, we will ask for your consent first.
  - heading: Changes to this policy
    body: |
      We may update this page when the site or applicable law changes. The “last updated” date at the top reflects the latest revision.
  - heading: Contact
    body: |
      Questions about privacy? Write to [solrisesoprano@gmail.com](mailto:solrisesoprano@gmail.com).
