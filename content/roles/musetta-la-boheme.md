Musetta is one of the central lyric roles in Puccini’s *La Bohème* — charismatic, witty, and vocally brilliant in the famous waltz *Quando m’en vo*. Sol Risé performs the role with Ópera de San Telmo at Teatro Maipú in Banfield.
